# Cutting Shrink 20% Through Investigations That Became Prevention

**Jordan S. Williams** — Asset Protection Investigator
Walmart, Lake Jackson, TX | 2022–2023

> The best investigation doesn't just close a case — it fixes the vulnerability that created it.

## The Challenge

Shrink from internal theft, external theft, and organized retail crime was driving measurable loss — and with it, operational risk to the store's people, assets, and profitability. The need wasn't just more apprehensions; it was a system that prevented loss while keeping customer and associate interactions professional and trust-based.

## What I Did

I treated every case as both an investigation and a diagnostic:

- **Led end-to-end investigations** across internal theft, external theft, and organized retail crime — reviewing CCTV footage and transactional activity to identify suspicious behavior, establish timelines, and build evidence to standard.
- **Conducted apprehensions and interviews** per established procedures, documenting violations, evidence, recoveries, and statements with the precision required for management and law-enforcement review.
- **Analyzed loss trends and inventory discrepancies** to surface patterns and operational vulnerabilities — turning individual incidents into insight about where the system was leaking.
- **Ran safety and security audits** and supported physical security and access-control procedures, identifying policy violations, unauthorized access, and conditions contributing to risk.
- **Coordinated with local law enforcement** on criminal investigations, apprehensions, evidence, and follow-up — maintaining professional communication and accurate documentation throughout.
- **Trained 15+ associates** on asset protection awareness, theft prevention, safety procedures, and appropriate customer interaction — then partnered with store leadership to build preventative strategies covering theft, safety, policy compliance, and operational risk.

## The Outcome

**Reduced store shrink by 20%** through targeted investigations, loss analysis, and preventative strategy.

The deeper win: the store didn't just lose less — it operated with more awareness. Associates were trained, vulnerabilities were addressed at the source, and leadership had a clearer picture of risk.

## Why It Matters

Asset protection taught me to lead with both accountability and coaching: investigate thoroughly, document accurately, train people well, and fix the system — not just the symptom. That discipline — data paired with people, enforcement paired with development — is exactly how I run operations today for a 10,000+ resident community.
