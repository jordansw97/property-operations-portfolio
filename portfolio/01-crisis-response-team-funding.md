# Securing a Crisis Response Team for Clute, Texas

**Jordan S. Williams** — Firefighter, Field Training Officer & Public Information Officer
City of Clute Fire Department, Clute, TX | 2018–2022

> Leadership is seeing the call behind the call — and building the system to answer it.

## The Challenge

Mental health-related calls were rising, and our traditional fire/EMS response wasn't the right tool for every crisis. Residents in crisis needed a more appropriate, safer response. Crews needed better options. The department needed a dedicated capability — but that required City Council funding and community trust.

## What I Did

As a firefighter serving simultaneously as Field Training Officer and Public Information Officer, I worked from both ends of the problem — operations and public trust:

- **Partnered with command staff** to define the operational gap: what kinds of calls were increasing, what a dedicated crisis response team would require, and how it would integrate with fire, EMS, and law enforcement.
- **Helped build the case for Council** — translating frontline experience into a clear, credible proposal that connected community need, responder safety, and service quality.
- **Presented alongside command staff to City Council** — speaking from the frontline perspective of someone who had mentored 10+ probationary firefighters through structured onboarding, evaluations, and coaching.
- **Protected public confidence as PIO** — serving as the department's primary spokesperson during emergency incidents, coordinating consistent messaging with incident command, partner agencies, and local media so the community understood not just what we were doing, but why.

## The Outcome

**City Council funded a dedicated crisis response team** for mental health-related calls — giving Clute a more appropriate response for vulnerable residents and a safer operating picture for responders.

Beyond the funding itself: the department strengthened its standing as a trusted, community-centered agency, and our crews gained a capability that matched the reality of the calls they were running.

## Why It Matters

This is the core of how I lead: stay calm under pressure, see the systemic need early, build the coalition to fund it, and communicate it in a way that earns trust. From emergency response to residential operations, I focus on the same things — developing people, strengthening systems, and creating measurable impact through accountability.
